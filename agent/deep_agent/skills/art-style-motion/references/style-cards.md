# Art style cards

Condensed English adaptations of the 35 recipe cards and index by alchaincyf (花叔 · 花生),
[huashu-art-motion d861767d180008d27675819932070a670a3ae43f](https://github.com/alchaincyf/huashu-art-motion/tree/d861767d180008d27675819932070a670a3ae43f/references),
read 2026-10-10. Preserve the MIT notice below and
[licence](https://github.com/alchaincyf/huashu-art-motion/blob/d861767d180008d27675819932070a670a3ae43f/LICENSE).
Upstream numbering omits 07. No upstream characters, sprites or demo media are supplied.

Each row describes a frame, motion brief and review risk. Procedural effects require an authored
asset; these cards are not executable templates for the current Remotion or HyperFrames tools.
Use broad visual characteristics and original subjects. Named contemporary aesthetics do not
grant rights to branded characters, reference images or proprietary artwork.

| Card | Palette and material | Living elements and boundary | Inspect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 Cave painting | Ochre, charcoal, rough rock; irregular hand-drawn animal silhouettes | Two-pose animals and firelight; opening in the rock | Figures must retain rough simplicity rather than cartoon polish. |
| 02 Egyptian mural | Flat earth colors, black contours, profile faces with frontal eyes | Scarab pushes a sun; rays and hieroglyphs; hanging mural strips | Elbows, pose joints and readable icon spacing. |
| 03 Greek pottery | Black silhouettes on terracotta, scratched lines, meander border | Border scrolls and a boat rocks; pottery rolls into view | Thin clothing details should not clog the silhouette. |
| 04 Roman mosaic | Tesserae follow contours; mortar and fixed irregular tiles | Color travels along a border; a wave of tile flips | Faces need enough tile resolution. Keep the tessellation stable. |
| 05 Gothic illumination | Parchment, embossed gold leaf, offset highlight edges | Gold glints, vines and a snail; page turn | Distinguish gilding from a flat yellow hood or hair. |
| 06 Renaissance | Warm glazed canvas, sfumato, fine crackle | Moving window light, dust, reflections or fluttering mechanisms | Avoid an inert painting or a cartoon character pasted on oil paint. |
| 08 Impressionism | Pastel regional palettes, short stable strokes | Sun flecks, poppies and cloud ribbons; paint points fill the frame | Do not fragment hands and faces beyond recognition. |
| 09 Van Gogh / post-impressionism | Regional flow fields, layered brush passes; sky #22339a/#2f4fb8/#8fb6ee, wall #7f9be6, floor #c0573c | Swirls, highlights and rings; vortex boundary | Split props into layers; oversized strokes destroy them. Never repaint every pixel randomly. |
| 10 Art Nouveau / Mucha | Cream and gold, variable-width contours, flowers and ornamental halos | Hair waves, flowers open, halos turn; outlined organic growth | The actor should move with the curved ornament. |
| 11 Cubism | Brown planes, translucent facets, angular dual viewpoints | Facets slide and newspaper trembles; fragments rearrange | Large dark planes are needed for structure, not only small triangles. |
| 12 Bauhaus | Paper #ebe3cf, red #d3262b, blue #2a58b4, yellow #efc12f, black #161514 | Pointer rotates, grids change, a ball rolls; geometric expansion | Actor anchors and geometric alignment must remain stable. |
| 13 Pop art | Bright flat colors, heavy black outlines and Ben-Day dots | Four-panel color changes and comic text beats; lightning/dot reveal | Use original motifs; avoid copied logos or comic heroes. |
| 14 8-bit | Reference pixel grid 240×135, integer pixels, nearest-neighbor, limited 14-color palette | Stepped 15–30 fps sprites, clouds, coins and HUD; pixel expansion | No antialiased edges; preserve collision and ground contact. |
| 15 Early ray-traced CGI | Plastic, chrome, checker reflections | Spinning ring and moving light; green wireframe/scanline reveal | Watch stiff faces and masks; references are not reusable assets. |
| 16 Contemporary flat animation | Flat color, light grain, little or no outline | Acted poses with damped secondary action; three-color concentric iris | Create an original actor rather than borrowing the demo character. |
| 17 Chinese ink | Wash, dry brush edges, generous white space and a seal | Brush reveal, ink diffusion and fine whisker movement; spreading wash | Actor movement needs intent rather than a stiff sliding cutout. |
| 18 Klimt | Gold leaf, spirals and eye patterns, modeled face against flat patterned clothing | Eyes blink, gold flakes shimmer; gold spiral reveal | Check sleeve/body joins and the face's contrast. |
| 19 Munch | Curved world shapes, blood-red sky, pressure in color bands | Sky flows and space warps; sweeping color bands | Use spatial distortion rather than copying Van Gogh brush swirls. |
| 20 Dunhuang | Mineral earth/red colors; distress on the background plaster | Ribbons fly, flames and flowers move; ribbon sweep/plaster fall | Keep damage off the actor and preserve silhouette contrast. |
| 21 Kusama | Irregular dots on a coherent repeating field | Radial dots pulse, pumpkin/mirror motifs; growing dot mask | Foreground and wall need different colors; preserve pattern period. |
| 22 Constructivism | Grayscale photographic texture, halftone, red wedges, black circles, stamped type | Letter stamps and geometric changes organize the argument | Keep evidence and type readable; do not fabricate documentary imagery. |
| 23 Dalí | Soft clocks, warped planes and long shadows | Clocks melt, ants move, shadows stretch; dripping boundary | A clean vector actor needs a deliberate treatment to belong. |
| 24 Hopper | Hard-edged light/shadow, separately clipped lit and shaded layers | Sunlight travels with linked shadows; sunlight sweep | Keep faces and important small subjects visible in shadow. |
| 25 Ghibli-like watercolor | Transparent wet-edge wash, layered background, cel shadows | Curtains, grass and patches of light; wet-paper diffusion | Use original characters; match their finish to the background. |
| 26 Vaporwave | Neon, VHS channel separation and a perspective grid | Grid scrolls, sun stripes and signs flicker; VHS tear/scroll | Excessive glow and intersecting grid lines muddy the frame. |
| 27 Kirby-like comics | Four-color halftone, ink misregistration, black energy dots and hatching | Callouts, panels and energy bursts; comic-panel reveal | Perspective and energetic shape distinguish it from generic pop art. |
| 28 Monet water lilies | Water #5b6fb5/#7d8fd0/#9db5e0, leaves #5f8f4a/#7fae5a, accents #f0a0b8/#f7d0dc; short horizontal strokes without outlines | Lilies drift, willow reflections ripple; ripple boundary | Render the bridge separately with contrast; no whole-screen texture boil. |
| 29 Seurat | Optical mixing with a restrained 21-color palette and complementary points | Light or point density changes; dots crystallize | Avoid a sterile regular grid; retain shapes through color mixing. |
| 30 Matisse cut paper | Saturated flat paper, imperfect cut edges and small layer shadows | Paper floats or seaweed sways; cut-paper sweep | Keep the material paper rather than adding paint texture. |
| 31 Haring | Thick consistent black contours and energy marks | Stepped 8 fps dances on the beat; dancer-shaped zoom mask | Preserve the simple silhouette rather than introducing realistic anatomy. |
| 32 Rembrandt | Warm chiaroscuro, multiplicative light over a modeled scene | Candle/window light, dust and cloud shadows; light iris | A flat face needs modeling, not only a dark overlay. |
| 33 Rubber hose 1930s | Pie eyes, noodle limbs, gloves and film grain | 12 fps beat-driven bounces; old-film iris | Acting must exist; moving grain alone does not animate the scene. |
| 34 Shadow puppet | Translucent hide, cutouts, joint rivets and lamplight | 10 fps rod/joint movement; backlight ignites | Overlapping translucent layers can become too dark. |
| 35 Shinkai-like light | Layered clouds, sunbeams, cel shadows and halos | Cloud layers and particles move; angled light sweep | Detailed interiors and original subjects must support the lighting. |
| 36 Picasso blue period | Elongated forms, monochrome blue, broad strokes and dark blue contour | Restrained movement; color drains before a brush wipe | Expressive form matters more than a blue filter. |

Choose one dominant material system. In a multi-world walk, change cards at boundaries while
preserving actor scale and gait. For another named style, create a separate original card with
palette, texture, motion, transition and risk, and identify its references and rights.
